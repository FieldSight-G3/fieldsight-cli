I'm Renata Salas, Safety Coordinator for the Meridian Utilities Fairfax substation.
On the morning of February 25, 2026, Marcus Whitfield, one of our relay technicians, was racking a 12.47 kV circuit breaker into its cubicle in
the switchgear lineup. The cubicle bus was energized at the time per the work order — this was a live racking operation, not de-energized or locked out,

transported to Inova Fairfax's burn unit the same day, February 25, 2026, where he was formally admitted as an in-patient — not held for observation — for
burn treatment and monitoring for smoke inhalation.
