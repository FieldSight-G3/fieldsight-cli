# Sprint 2 retro

## What went well

We organized ourselves well this sprint. Having adjusted to the scope of the project, everyone knew their tasks and was able to work on them independently.

## What didn't

Merging our individual changes was much harder this sprint, because we had to understand how our implementations connected to each other. Integrating them meant reading and understanding each other's code, and the merged result still had to pass all of our checks: lint, tests, and secret scan.

Sprint 2's workload was also much heavier than our other sprints. Going forward, we need to be stricter with ourselves about finishing each sprint's committed work.

## One concrete change for next sprint

Before building, each of us will identify where our code connects to a teammate's implementation and take time to understand what their code does at that point, so integration happens as we build rather than at merge time.
