Supervisor Statement — Incident INC-0412
 
Date of statement: 2 March 2026

I'm Renata Salas, Safety Coordinator for the Meridian Utilities Fairfax substation. 
On the morning of February 25, 2026, Marcus Whitfield, one of our relay technicians, was racking a 12.47 kV circuit breaker into its cubicle in 
the switchgear lineup. The cubicle bus was energized at the time per the work order — this was a live racking operation, not de-energized or locked out, 
since the breaker needed to be placed into service under load.
As Marcus operated the manual racking mechanism to move the breaker into the connected position, an arc flash occurred inside the cubicle. 
He sustained electrical burns to both hands and forearms and was exposed to smoke from the arc. We called an ambulance immediately, and he was 
transported to Inova Fairfax's burn unit, where he was formally admitted as an in-patient — not held for observation — for burn treatment and 
monitoring for smoke inhalation. I notified our safety hotline of the in-patient hospitalization the same day, within the hour of learning he'd 
been admitted. Marcus returned to full duty on August 21, 2026.
