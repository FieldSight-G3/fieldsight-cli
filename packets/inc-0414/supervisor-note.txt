Supervisor Statement — Incident INC-0414

Date of statement: 22 July 2026

I'm Renata Salas, Safety Coordinator for the Meridian Utilities Fairfax substation. On the afternoon of July 14, 2026, it was around 97°F with a heat advisory in effect. 
Thomas Baptiste and I were moving cable reels between the laydown area and the truck out in the yard, in direct sun with no shade cover nearby. Around 1:30 pm he told me 
he felt dizzy and his chest felt tight, and he sat down against the fence line while I called it in.
We got him to the Sentara Northern Virginia Medical Center emergency department the same afternoon, July 14, 2026. The doctors kept him overnight to run cardiac tests and 
monitor him, but he wasn't admitted for any treatment — the discharge paperwork was clear that it was observation only. The cardiac tests came back clear and the ER physician 
diagnosed heat exhaustion. 

He was discharged and sent home the morning of July 15 and did not work that day.
He came back on July 16 on light duty — no outdoor work and no heavy lifting, per the ER physician's note — through July 20. He returned to full duty on July 21, 2026.
