Supervisor statement - incident 2026-0413

Supervisor: R. Okafor, yard crew lead

The employee was unloading a used wooden crossarm from the back of the yard
truck so it could go to the scrap pile. The end of the arm was split and
weathered. As he lowered it to the gravel, the split end caught the palm of
his right hand and drove a splinter about an inch long into it. He was
wearing cotton gloves, not the leather pair.

He finished securing the load and came to the office. We could see the end
of the splinter but it was too deep to pull out safely with tweezers. He was
seen at the Alder Ridge Occupational clinic on 17 March 2026, where the
clinician removed it through a small incision and prescribed an antibiotic.

Nothing electrical was involved. He was not admitted anywhere and has had no
time away from work and no restrictions.
