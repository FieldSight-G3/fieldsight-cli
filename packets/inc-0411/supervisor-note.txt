Supervisor Statement — Incident INC-0411
 
Date of statement: 10 September 2026

I'm Renata Salas, Safety Coordinator for the Ashburn Service Center. On the morning of September 9, 2026, I was on site when Daniel Ortiz, one of our field technicians, 
was injured while securing cable tray brackets in the substation control building. Daniel was using a hand ratchet to tighten conduit clamps along a tray run when his hand 
slipped off the ratchet handle. His forearm came down against the sharp edge of a clamp that hadn't been fully seated yet, and it opened a cut on his left forearm.
 
I checked on him right away, applied a pressure bandage from the first-aid kit, and had him driven to the Inova Loudoun Hospital emergency department that same morning. 
He was seen, given sutures, and released the same day — no admission, no overnight stay. He was back at work the next shift with no restrictions. This was a mechanical 
handling incident only; no energized equipment or electrical conductors were involved in the control building work at the time.
